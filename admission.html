Registration & Admission Procedure

Registrations for admission start from the Last Week of February. Candidates must appear for a written test. On satisfactory clearance, the child is admitted to the school.

Nursery Section

The age limit for admission as of 1st April is:

  • L.K.G. : 4 Years & above
  • U.K.G. : 5 Years & above

Junior Section

For Class 1, the age limit is 6 Years+. For every class above, a difference of one year is required. Written tests cover:

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Hindi
