Our Excellence

Kanpur Public School is located in the heart of Kannauj City at Bada Bazar. KPS is established by a leading Kanpur based educational group to serve the world class educational facilities for the community of Kannauj. KPS aims to provide best of educational facilities through English as the medium of instruction without discrimination of religion, cast, creed & colour and preparing conscientious and responsible citizens, with a sense of integrity and honesty through intellectual, moral & physical developments.
