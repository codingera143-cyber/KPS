Words of Wisdom

Message from the Principal

As a principal of Kanpur Public School of Kannauj. My primary focus is student learning. My objective is to explore hidden talents of my students. All students must be prepared to be successful in the 21st century careers. Students must know how to think, to solve problems, to innovate and to collaborate There is no longer a single pathway to that end but what ever paths our students choose must be steeped with efforts, dedication and relevance. Students must be provided with authentic learning experiences and they must be connected to adults who truly care. As the adults in their lives, we must be provided guidance in the development of character. We must help the students to act with compassion, accept responsibility and choose to do right things. Our philosophy of education has always been meeting of the individual child. Children acquire skills and knowledge easily if we can make the surroundings stimulating and purposeful.

In such surroundings when work and effort is valued, the child’s self esteem is brightened and from this result self- motivation. In our school, we provide such atmosphere to our students so that their mental skills are broaden. We create play and learn atmosphere also for our little students through experimental teaching.
